What we do

From first idea to finished book.

You do not need to coordinate five different vendors to publish one exceptional book. Words brings the essential creative and production disciplines together under one roof.

01

Ghostwriting

When the story is in your head but the manuscript is not yet on the page, we help give it a voice. Our writers work from your ideas, expertise, interviews and direction to create a manuscript that sounds intentional and authentic.

↗
02

Editing

Developmental and line editing that strengthens structure, argument, pacing, clarity and voice without sanding away what makes the work yours.

↗
03

Proofreading

A disciplined final pass for grammar, spelling, punctuation, consistency and presentation so your manuscript is ready for publication with confidence.

↗
04

Book formatting

Interior layouts designed for comfortable reading and professional production across print and digital editions, with careful attention to hierarchy, margins, typography and front matter.

↗
05

Book cover design

A cover should communicate the character of the book before a reader opens it. We create distinctive, market-aware covers that respect both the story and the audience.

↗
06

Book publishing

We guide the final production and publishing process so your completed work can move from files to a properly prepared, professional publication.
